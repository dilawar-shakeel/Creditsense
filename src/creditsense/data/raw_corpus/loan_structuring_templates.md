# Part A: Term Loan Templates

## Template T-1: Micro Enterprise Term Loan — Bank-Statement-Only

Facility type: Term loan, up to PKR 3,000,000, tenor up to 3 years. Documentation: 12
months bank statements per Policy P-7. Security: personal guarantee, within the
Regulation R-9 clean exposure ceiling. Pricing: KIBOR + risk-adjusted spread per the
applicant's credit score under Regulation R-15.

## Template T-2: Small Enterprise Term Loan — Unaudited Financials

Facility type: Term loan, up to PKR 30,000,000, tenor up to 5 years. Documentation:
unaudited financials cross-checked per Policy P-8. Security: minimum 0.50 collateral
coverage per Policy P-24, or clean up to the Regulation R-9 ceiling if unsecured.

## Template T-3: Medium Enterprise Term Loan — Audited Financials

Facility type: Term loan, no tier-based facility cap (subject to the Regulation R-5
per-party ceiling). Documentation: statutory audited financials per Policy P-9. Security:
registered mortgage and/or hypothecation per Policy P-23, valued per Annexure-II to
Regulation R-17.

## Template T-4: Start-up Term Loan

Facility type: Term loan, up to PKR 5,000,000, tenor up to 3 years, available to any
enterprise five years old or less per the SBP Start-up definition. Underwriting relies
more heavily on income estimation models per Regulation R-1(xi), given limited credit
history.

## Template T-5: Green/Energy-Efficiency Term Loan

Facility type: Term loan for captive power or energy-efficiency capital expenditure, up
to PKR 20,000,000. Primarily targeted at the Textile/Garments and Steel/Re-rolling
sector overlays (Policy P-12, P-13) given their elevated load-shedding exposure.

# Part B: Working Capital Templates

## Template T-6: Running Finance — Standard

Facility type: Revolving running finance against hypothecation of stock and
receivables, limit set at 20% of annual bank turnover, adjusted per the risk multiplier
described in the Bank's credit scoring methodology (Policy P-10). Annual review
required to re-verify tier classification per Policy P-6.

## Template T-7: Seasonal Working Capital — Agri-Processing

Facility type: Running finance with a seasonal drawdown/repayment schedule aligned
to harvest cycles, per the Rice/Agri-processing sector overlay in Policy P-19. Limit
reviewed each season against realized purchase volumes.

## Template T-8: Export Working Capital

Facility type: Pre-shipment and post-shipment finance for exporting SMEs, available at
preferential pricing where export sales exceed 40% of turnover per the
Leather/Sports Goods overlay in Policy P-17, extendable to other export-oriented
sectors on a case-by-case basis.

## Template T-9: Digital Supply Chain Finance

Facility type: Short-tenor invoice/purchase-order finance delivered through the Bank's
Digital Supply Chain Financing platform under Regulation R-14(b), available to SMEs
onboarded via a validated anchor (manufacturer, distributor, or digital platform).

## Template T-10: Clean Working Capital Facility

Facility type: Unsecured running finance against personal guarantee only, capped at the
Regulation R-9 clean exposure ceiling of PKR 50,000,000, excluding any clean consumer
financing already extended to the sponsor.

# Part C: Trade Finance Templates

## Template T-11: Letter of Credit — Import

Facility type: Sight or usance import LC, primarily for FX-import-dependent sectors
(Auto Parts/Engineering per Policy P-16, Steel/Re-rolling per Policy P-13, Pharma/
Surgical per Policy P-20). Requires the FX hedging policy statement described in Policy
P-13 where applicable.

## Template T-12: Letter of Guarantee — Performance

Facility type: Performance guarantee for construction and supply contracts, targeted at
the Construction/Building Materials overlay (Policy P-14). Underwriting includes the
buyer concentration disclosure required by that policy.

## Template T-13: Inland Bill Discounting

Facility type: Discounting of inland trade bills. Classification follows the accelerated
Loss-category trigger in Annexure-I to Regulation R-17 for bills unpaid 180 days past
due, distinct from the standard one-year trigger for term facilities.

# Part D: Group and Associate Structuring Templates

## Template T-14: Group Facility — Aggregated Limit

Facility type: A single facility structure spanning an applicant and its identified
group/associate companies (Policy P-28), with the aggregate limit tested against the
Regulation R-5 per-party ceiling before incremental approval per Policy P-29.

## Template T-15: Associate Company Carve-out

Facility type: Standalone facility to an associate company where the group's aggregate
exposure has already reached the Regulation R-5 ceiling — permissible only where the
associate can independently qualify without reliance on group creditworthiness, per
Policy P-29's headroom calculation.

## Template T-16: Related-Party Exclusion Checklist

Not a financing structure but a mandatory pre-approval checklist confirming the
applicant is screened against Regulation R-11's related-party restriction (Policy P-30)
before any group or associate facility is submitted for approval.

# Part E: Restructuring Templates

## Template T-17: Standard Restructuring — Cash Recovery Path

Facility type: Restructuring conditioned on at least 10% cash recovery of outstanding
principal and six months of sustained compliance, per Regulation R-18(i) and Policy
P-34, before classification may improve.

## Template T-18: Accelerated Restructuring — 35% Recovery Path

Facility type: Restructuring where 35% of outstanding principal and mark-up is
recovered in cash at or before the six-month mark, allowing classification improvement
immediately upon that recovery per Regulation R-18(i).

## Template T-19: Working Capital Restructuring

Facility type: Restructuring that includes fresh working capital, evaluated strictly on
business viability per Regulation R-18(vi) and monitored separately from the restructured
facility per Regulation R-18(iv).

# Part F: Documentation Checklists by Tier

## Template T-20: Documentation Checklist — Micro Enterprise

Required at origination: CNIC, 12 months bank statements, standardized loan
application form per Regulation R-6, e-CIB report per Regulation R-7, KYC per Policy
P-37.

## Template T-21: Documentation Checklist — Small Enterprise

Required at origination: everything in Template T-20, plus unaudited financial
statements, collateral valuation where secured (Policy P-26), and guarantor net worth
verification where applicable (Policy P-25).

## Template T-22: Documentation Checklist — Medium Enterprise

Required at origination: everything in Template T-21, plus statutory audited financial
statements, group/associate exposure declaration (Policy P-28), and sector overlay
documentation from Part C of the internal credit policy where applicable.

## Template T-23: Digital Onboarding Checklist

Required where onboarding is conducted digitally under Regulation R-14(a): e-agreement
execution via secure digital signature, Video KYC or Geo-tagged on-boarding data,
reused account-opening data per Policy P-38, and third-party sourcing of statutory
data per Policy P-39 wherever available.
